My Story

From editing videos to building business systems.

I started with video editing, learning how pacing, captions, music, and B-roll keep people watching. Then I started working with AI content, product ads, and VSL-style videos, where every second needs to support attention and conversion.

Today, I combine creative execution with technical systems: GoHighLevel, automation workflows, website/funnel design, content repurposing, and AI-assisted production.
